EV Route Planner is an India-first EV journey intelligence engine designed to determine the safest, most efficient, and most reliable way for an electric vehicle to complete a journey.

Rather than simply locating charging stations or calculating the shortest route, the engine combines route information, vehicle characteristics, energy consumption, charging infrastructure, station reliability, and charging strategy to produce and evaluate complete EV journey plans.

The project follows a validation-first approach: the intelligence engine will be benchmarked against existing solutions before significant investment is made in external providers or an Android application.
